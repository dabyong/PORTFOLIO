PROJECT 01 · BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Olist E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Revenue Analytics

An end-to-end analysis of historical Brazilian e-commerce transactions. I profiled and validated raw CSV datasets with Python, loaded and modeled the data in MySQL, built reusable SQL analyses, and created a four-page Power BI dashboard with business recommendations.

Key finding: Late deliveries had an average order-level review score of 2.27/5, compared with 4.29/5 for on-time or early deliveries. This is an observed association, not a causal claim.

PROJECT 02 · CUSTOMER ANALYTICS & APPLIED MACHINE LEARNING

Customer Journey & Conversion Funnel Analytics

An end-to-end analysis of Google Analytics 4 sample e-commerce events. I used BigQuery SQL to model ordered session-level funnel stages, built an interactive Power BI dashboard, tested the December–January completion decline with Python bootstrapping, and used an exploratory Isolation Forest model to flag unusual daily activity.

56,696Product-view sessions
4.85%Strict funnel completion*
5 / 68Days flagged by ML

Key finding: Product view to cart was the largest funnel drop-off (74.18%). An exploratory anomaly model flagged five days for investigation, not five confirmed business incidents.
